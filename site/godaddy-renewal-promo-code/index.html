Build a renewal evidence sheet before paying

This is PerkMingle's editorial worksheet, not an additional GoDaddy rule. Fill it from your own offer and quote; an empty field means unanswered, not eligible. Keep the sheet private and leave out account numbers and payment details.

  1. Name the transaction. Write down the product, domain extension if relevant, renewal period, currency and quote date. Label it renewal, new registration or transfer before comparing any prices.
  2. Locate the renewal wording. Record the exact sentence in your offer that includes renewals. If you only have a headline advertising a saving, leave eligibility unresolved and ask support.
  3. Check the scope. Note the product and term named in the offer, any expiry, and whether it is addressed to you. Write “not stated” for missing details rather than supplying your own deadline.
  4. Keep a matching baseline. Record the total without the offer and the total with it for the same product and period. Keep taxes, fees and optional extras visible in both versions.
  5. Separate a rejection from a missing saving. Copy the displayed error, or note that the total did not change. Neither observation tells you why it happened; ask for an explanation instead of declaring the code expired.
  6. Make the decision from the payable total. A larger advertised percentage is not useful evidence when the product, term or included extras changed. Compare like with like before choosing whether to renew.

Compare costs without inventing a renewal price

Use your actual, current quotes rather than a first-year headline or a price somebody else paid. Choose one comparison period and currency. Include only services you intend to keep, and record what happens after that period separately.

Observed saving = matching total without the offer − matching total with the offer. Both inputs must come from your own quote for the same basket. If either total is missing, the saving is unknown. This is a calculation method, not a claim that a discount will be available.

For any paid membership you are considering, compare its full fee plus the quoted renewals against the same renewals without membership. Do not assume that holding more domains makes membership cheaper: insert the quotes for your own extensions and quantities. Leave this comparison unfinished if you cannot obtain both totals.

If you consider moving a domain, create a separate comparison using the receiving registrar's official transfer quote, included period and subsequent renewal quote. Do not put an introductory transfer amount in the renewal-price column. This worksheet recommends no registrar and supplies no transfer price.

A precise question for support

Please check whether my renewal is eligible for a current offer. Does the offer apply to this product and renewal term? Please confirm its expiry, exclusions, and the total payable including fees and taxes. If it does not apply, please identify the condition that excludes it.

Send this through GoDaddy's own support channel after checking the official site. Keep the answer with the matching quote. Do not publish your account-specific offer as a code that everybody can use.
