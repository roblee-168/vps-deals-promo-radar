Where offers come from

We check public official provider pages on a schedule of 6 hours. Requests respect robots.txt and do not bypass access controls. A listing means a promotional heading was found on the official page; it does not mean we tested checkout or verified eligibility for every visitor.

What we publish

Titles come from source headings. Prices are included only when a matching named offer contains structured price and currency data. Credits are never presented as purchase prices. Unknown dates, discounts and prices are omitted.

Freshness

At each build, failed sources are removed, expired promotions are excluded and records older than 24 hours are omitted. If scheduled checks or deployment stop, the static site cannot refresh itself: always review the displayed source-check timestamp. The source table includes unsuccessful checks.

Affiliate disclosure

Some future links may earn us a commission after an approved affiliate partnership is configured. Each offer states whether its link is direct or affiliated. Paid relationships do not determine listing order. We do not claim unverified commissions or income.

Your privacy

This site has no account system, forms or advertising trackers. The hosting provider may process access logs. External providers apply their own privacy and purchase terms when you follow their links.
