Hostinger domain coupon code: what is verified?

No coupon code for a standalone domain purchase was verified in the official pages checked on 2026-09-28. Hostinger publishes hosting coupons and a free-first-year domain benefit with eligible plans. Those are different from a verified domain-only code. Start with the official free-domain conditions or domain pricing page.

Choose the route that matches your purchase

Our editorial suggestion: if you only need a domain, compare its full registration term and renewal quote. If you also need hosting, compare the entire hosting commitment rather than treating the bundled domain as a standalone discount. Neither route establishes that a hosting coupon applies to a domain-only basket.

This guide concerns digital domain services. We verified no shipping-threshold offer or separate domain membership discount in the linked sources and include neither as a benefit.

Frequently asked questions

Is the included domain free forever?
