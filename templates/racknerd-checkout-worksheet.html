Why these checks matter

RackNerd's ordering guide walks through configuration, billing-cycle choice, and reviewing the order summary. The public cart includes a code field and a totals area. Neither page says that the dedicated-server banner code qualifies for a VPS special.

RackNerd's refund article says it does not offer refunds or a money-back guarantee. It suggests a monthly purchase and its Looking Glass if you want to test before a longer commitment. That advice does not turn an annual special into a monthly plan.

Decision rule: if the product class, final amount, or renewal terms remain unclear, pause before payment and ask RackNerd to confirm them. The worksheet records your own checkout result; it does not submit any data or place an order.
