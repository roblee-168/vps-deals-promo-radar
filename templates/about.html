OUR METHOD

About PerkMingle

PerkMingle is operated by Jiawei Li. It is a directory of official VPS promotions and trials, built to help readers find the original offer and understand what has and has not been checked. PerkMingle does not sell hosting or manage customers' servers.

For corrections or questions, contact PerkMingle.

Where offers come from

We check public official provider pages on a schedule of $hours hours. Requests respect robots.txt and do not bypass access controls. A listing means we found a qualifying promotion, trial or credit on a public official page. It does not mean we tested checkout or verified eligibility for every visitor.

What we publish

We read official headings, structured offer data and supported VPS pricing cards. When a pricing card states an initial price, billing period or renewal price, we label those separately. A monthly rate tied to a longer term is not a month-to-month purchase price. Credits are never presented as purchase prices. Missing terms are marked as not specified or omitted; we do not calculate or invent savings.

Freshness

At each build, failed sources are removed, expired promotions are excluded and records older than $age hours are omitted. If scheduled checks or deployment stop, the static site cannot refresh itself: always review the displayed source-check timestamp. The source table includes unsuccessful checks.

Affiliate disclosure

RackNerd plan links currently include affiliate tracking. We may earn a commission if you purchase through those links, at no extra cost to you. Each offer states whether its link is direct or affiliated. Paid relationships do not determine listing order. We do not claim unverified commissions or income.

Your privacy

This site has no account system or forms. Cloudflare provides hosting and web analytics; the delivered site includes its analytics beacon. Links to hosting providers may include affiliate tracking, as disclosed on the relevant page. The home page displays an official RackNerd affiliate banner. Read our privacy policy for the current details. External providers apply their own privacy and purchase terms when you follow their links.
